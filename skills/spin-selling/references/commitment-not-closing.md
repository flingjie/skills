# Commitment, Not Closing

In complex sales the goal is not an immediate close — it is the next valuable
action.

## The commitment ladder

```text
Demo
  ↓
技术评估
  ↓
POC
  ↓
业务访谈
  ↓
内部方案讨论
  ↓
预算申请
  ↓
采购流程
```

## Every conversation must end with three things

1. 下一步是什么（what）
2. 谁负责（who）
3. 什么时候（when）

Example:

- 下一步：安排技术团队一起做需求梳理
- 谁：你负责拉产品负责人，我负责准备方案
- 何时：下周二之前完成

## Anti-pattern

> "那您再考虑一下。"

No owner, no deadline, no action. A commitment has all three.

## Rule

Match the commitment to the need-development stage. If the need is not yet
developed, the next step is a discovery question — not a POC. If the need is
fully developed and the value is stated by the customer, push for a concrete
next action with owner and deadline.
