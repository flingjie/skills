# Challenger Framework

From The Challenger Sale. Three capabilities separate a challenger from a
"relationship builder": **Teach, Tailor, Take Control.**

## Teach

Do not open with the product. Open with an insight the customer has not had:
something that challenges their framing and makes them rethink their own
problem. Teaching is not "educating about your product" — it is revealing a
hidden mechanism in the customer's own business.

## Tailor

The same insight must be re-expressed for the person in front of you. A CTO, a
CEO, and a product manager each care about a different consequence of the same
underlying problem. See `references/persona-tailoring.md`.

## Take Control

The insight and narrative are useless if the conversation does not move toward a
decision. A challenger presses for the next concrete step, names the blocker, and
drives to a committed action. See `references/deal-advancement.md`.

## The 6-Part Narrative

Build the narrative in this fixed order. Solution is always last.

### 1. Warmer

Establish relevance. Anchor to a situation the customer recognizes.

> 最近很多 AI Agent 团队都遇到一个类似问题……

### 2. Reframe

Challenge the current framing. Attribute the problem to a different cause than
the one they assumed.

> 大多数团队认为问题是 Prompt，但实际上 Prompt 往往只是问题暴露的位置。

### 3. Rational Drowning

Quantify the cost of staying the course — rationally, not fear-mongering.

> 如果每次 Agent 出错都依赖人工排查，随着 Agent 数量增加，
> Debug 成本会呈非线性增长。

### 4. Emotional Impact

Make the cost concrete and felt. Show the daily grind it produces.

> 最终工程师可能每天都在重复：
>
> 查看 Trace → 猜测失败原因 → 修改 Prompt → 重新运行 → 出现新的问题。

### 5. New Way

Propose a new model — a way of thinking about the solution, not the product yet.

> 真正需要的是：
>
> Observe → Classify → Replay → Evaluate → Learn

### 6. Solution

Only now, introduce the product, and only as the thing that operationalizes the
"New Way."

> 我们的平台提供了……

---

## Ordering rule

The narrative moves **away** from the customer's current frame and **toward** a
new one. If you find yourself describing the product in step 2, you have
skipped the reframe — start over.
