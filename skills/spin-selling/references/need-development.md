# Need Development

The real point of SPIN is not the questions — it is the customer's need
development (需求认知升级).

## The progression

```text
现在还可以用
  ↓
确实有点麻烦
  ↓
如果继续这样下去，问题会越来越严重
  ↓
如果能解决，确实很有价值
  ↓
那下一步怎么做？
```

Each stage maps to a SPIN type:

| Customer says | SPIN stage |
|---------------|-----------|
| 现在还可以用 | S |
| 确实有点麻烦 | P |
| 会越来越严重 | I |
| 很有价值 | N |
| 下一步怎么做 | Commitment |

## Determining the current stage

Read the customer's last statement and classify it. The stage is what the
customer now believes — not what you have said. If the customer is still "现在
还可以用", you are at S regardless of how much you have pitched.

## Generating the next question

If the customer is at:

- **S** → ask a Problem question (surface a problem in their situation).
- **P** → ask an Implication question (amplify, do not pitch).
- **I** → ask a Need-payoff question (turn urgency into a value they state).
- **N** → propose a Commitment (concrete next step).

## Never skip I

Jumping P → N (or P → pitch) leaves the problem small and the pitch premature.
Implication is the step that makes the problem feel worth solving.

## The rule of one

Ask one question at a time. Given the stage, the next question should be the
single most valuable one — not a list of five.
