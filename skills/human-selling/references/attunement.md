# Attunement

Attunement is entering the other person's world — seeing the situation from
their side before proposing anything.

## What to analyze

- WHO — who are they?
- ROLE — what is their role?
- GOAL — what do they want to achieve?
- PAIN — what problem are they hitting now?
- CONSTRAINT — what limits them?
- RISK — what are they worried about?
- ALTERNATIVE — how do they solve it today?
- INCENTIVE — why would they act?
- INERTIA — why might they not act?

## Rules

- Fill only what the user gave or what you can reasonably infer — mark inference
  as a hypothesis, not a fact.
- If a critical cell is empty and it changes the advice, ask before advising.
- Do not ask more than 3 questions at once.
- Attunement is continuous — update the model as the conversation reveals more.

## The core question

Not "how do I get them to say yes?" but "what does their world actually look
like right now?"
